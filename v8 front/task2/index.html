Ada Lovelace

The first computer programmer and visionary of general-purpose computing

Key Milestones

  • 1815: Born Augusta Ada Byron in London, England.
  • 1833: Met mathematician Charles Babbage and saw his Difference Engine.
  • 1843: Published extensive notes on the Analytical Engine, including Note G with an algorithm for computing Bernoulli numbers.
  • Legacy: Recognized that computing machines could manipulate symbols beyond pure arithmetic, laying conceptual foundations for modern software.
